01 / FIELD VALIDATION

I check my work in the field.

At UConn CIRCA, I built a computer vision pipeline that estimates flood depth from street photos. I checked its estimates against RTK field measurements.

21 field measurements across 19 locations

3.84 cm mean absolute error in a preliminary evaluation of 16 locations.

Road flood preparedness. Photo-based estimates helped local teams prioritize which flooded streets to check first. Separate high-tide forecasts supported resident alerts in Stonington, New London, and Groton.
